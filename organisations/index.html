How we work with you

Our matter-management process is designed for institutions that need accountability and a paper trail.

One designated contact
On appointment, the director is your single point of contact for every matter we handle for you.
Deadlines monitored
Each step on a file has a set time period, checked by the supervising director so nothing becomes time-barred.
Written reports
You receive a written update after every significant step, and a close-out report with the final account.
Your approval for costs
We seek your written approval before briefing counsel or experts. In an emergency we ask by phone and confirm in writing within 24 hours.
Billing that suits you
Accounts at the end of a matter, at agreed stages or monthly, as your finance processes require.
