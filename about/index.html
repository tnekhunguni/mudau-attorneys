About the firm

A general law practice in Thohoyandou, Limpopo, founded and directed by Makondelele Mudau. We act for individuals, businesses and public institutions across South Africa.

Makondelele Mudau

Director

Mr Mudau graduated with an LLB from the University of Venda in 2012 and completed his practical legal training at the Polokwane School for Legal Practice. He served his articles of clerkship with Legal Aid South Africa, then worked as a litigator with various attorneys and debt counsellors in Gauteng Province.

In 2015 he co-founded Mudau & Netshipise Attorneys, where he practised as a director until 2025. That year he opened Mudau Makondelele Attorneys in Thohoyandou, Limpopo Province.

His work covers labour and employment law, including appearances at the CCMA, bargaining councils and the Labour Court; criminal litigation; civil litigation; medical negligence; Road Accident Fund claims; unlawful arrest; constitutional and administrative law; and commercial and contract work. He has chaired and initiated disciplinary hearings, drafted procurement policies, and advised public bodies and private companies.

Mr Mudau has litigated in several courts and forums, including but not limited to the CCMA, bargaining councils, the Magistrates' Courts, the Regional Courts, the High Court, the Labour Court, the Labour Appeal Court, the Supreme Court of Appeal and the Constitutional Court.

He served the Black Lawyers Association in Gauteng as Head of Events and Campaigns and later as Treasurer.

The team

A team of fourteen, consisting of admitted attorneys, candidate attorneys, paralegals, administrative staff and an in-house bookkeeper who looks after the firm's trust and business accounts. Every matter is supervised by the director.

Your records are safe with us

Client files are kept in paper and electronic form. Electronic files are backed up regularly and held off site, so your matter is not lost or delayed if something goes wrong at the office. Personal information is handled under the Protection of Personal Information Act; see our privacy notice.

How your matter is handled

  1. First consultation

    We listen, take your instructions and explain your options, the likely steps and our fees.

  2. A named person on your file

    Your matter goes to the most suitable member of the team, supervised by the director. Every document carries the name of the person who prepared it.

  3. Written updates at every step

    You hear from us in writing after each step. We ask your permission before briefing an advocate or an expert.

  4. A close-out report

    When the matter ends, you receive a report on what was done and the result, with the final account.
