The Road Accident Fund (RAF) compensates people injured in road accidents caused by the negligent driving of someone else, and the dependants of people killed in them. Passengers, pedestrians and drivers who were not at fault can all claim. The most important thing to know is that **the claim expires if it is not lodged in time**.

## The time limits

- **The driver is known:** lodge the claim within **3 years** of the accident.
- **Hit-and-run (the driver or owner was never identified):** lodge within **2 years** of the accident.
- **After lodging:** if the claim is not settled, a summons must generally be issued within **5 years** of the accident.

Once a claim has prescribed, it cannot be revived, no matter how serious the injuries.

## What you can claim for

Depending on the circumstances: past and future medical expenses, loss of income or earning capacity, general damages for serious injuries, loss of support for dependants, and funeral costs.

## What to gather

1. The accident report and police case number.
2. Hospital and clinic records, and any medical reports.
3. Your ID and proof of income before the accident.
4. Names and contact details of witnesses.
