Privacy and POPIA notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Mudau Makondelele Attorneys (registration number 2025/416899/21) is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website and in the course of our legal practice. Our Information Officer is the Director, Makondelele Mudau.

Stand 709, Madanzhe Street, P West, Thohoyandou, Limpopo, 0950. Email info@mudauattorneys.com, telephone 015 023 1380.

What we collect

  • Details you give us through the contact form, by phone, WhatsApp or email: your name, contact details and a description of your matter.
  • Information needed to act for you once you instruct us, including identity documents required under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act.
  • Information about other people involved in your matter, where it is needed to give you legal services.
  • Basic technical information our web host records automatically, such as IP address and browser type, used to keep the website secure.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To give you legal advice and represent you.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including those under the Legal Practice Act, the Financial Intelligence Centre Act and tax law.
  • To bill for our services and manage our trust account.

We do not sell personal information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only where needed for your matter or required by law: courts and tribunals, advocates and experts we brief with your permission, opposing parties' legal representatives, regulators, and service providers who support our practice (such as our IT and backup providers) under confidentiality obligations.

Confidentiality and security

Communications with us about your legal matter are confidential and may be protected by legal professional privilege. We keep paper files secured at our offices and electronic files on access-controlled systems with regular off-site backups.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become matters are deleted within 12 months. Client files are kept for as long as the law and our professional rules require after the matter closes, then destroyed securely.

Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold personal information about you and give you a copy;
  • correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed;
  • stop processing your information, where the law allows.

Send requests to info@mudauattorneys.com. Some information may have to be kept, for example where the law requires it or where it is privileged.

Complaints

Please raise any concern with us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za.

Access to information (PAIA)

Our manual under the Promotion of Access to Information Act is available on request.
