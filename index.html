Makondelele Mudau

Director

Mr Mudau studied law at the University of Venda and completed his practical legal training at the Polokwane School for Legal Practice. He served his articles with Legal Aid South Africa, worked as a litigator in Gauteng Province, and spent ten years as a co-founder and director of Mudau & Netshipise Attorneys before opening this firm in Thohoyandou, Limpopo Province.

His practice covers labour and employment law, criminal and civil litigation, medical negligence, Road Accident Fund claims, unlawful arrest and commercial work. He has chaired disciplinary hearings, drafted procurement policies and advised public bodies and private companies.

He has litigated in forums from the CCMA and bargaining councils to the Supreme Court of Appeal and the Constitutional Court.

How your matter is handled

The same process applies whether you're an individual or an institution.

  1. First consultation

    We listen, take your instructions and explain your options, the likely steps and our fees.

  2. A named person on your file

    Your matter goes to the most suitable member of the team, supervised by the director. Every document carries the name of the person who prepared it.

  3. Written updates at every step

    You hear from us in writing after each step. We ask your permission before briefing an advocate or an expert.

  4. A close-out report

    When the matter ends, you receive a report on what was done and the result, with the final account.
